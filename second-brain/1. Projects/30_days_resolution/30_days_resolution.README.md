## 🗓️ **WEEK 2 — Build Automatic Speaking Patterns**

_Goal: turn grammar into reflex, not thinking._

### **Day 8:**

- [ ] Learn 5 auto-phrases
- [ ] Practice them in 10 short sentences

### **Day 9:**

- [ ] Shadowing 10 mins
- [ ] Speak: describe a picture or YouTube thumbnail

### **Day 10:**

3-2-1 Method (AMAZING for fluency):

- speak 3 min
    
- same topic 2 min
    
- same topic 1 min  
    Talk faster each time → fluency grows.
    

### **Day 11:**

Mini conversation with me (we can simulate small talk)

### **Day 12:**

Grammar-to-speech exercise:  
Take 5 grammar rules you know  
Turn them into short spoken examples

### **Day 13:**

“Thinking in English” practice for 10 minutes  
Describe everything around you in simple English

### **Day 14:**

**Review day**  
Record a 2-minute casual talk  
Compare with Week 1

---

## 🗓️ **WEEK 3 — Real Conversation Training**

_Goal: survive real-time English without freezing._

### **Day 15:**

Shadowing 10–12 min  
Practice conversation starters

### **Day 16:**

Speak about feelings:  
“I feel…”  
“I’m worried because…”  
“I’m excited about…”

### **Day 17:**

Practice work English  
Tell a 2-minute story from your job

### **Day 18:**

Q&A practice with me  
I ask → you answer quickly  
Focus: speed, not perfection

### **Day 19:**

Record: “My opinion about …”  
Choose a topic you like

### **Day 20:**

Watch a short English video  
Explain it in your own words (1 min)

### **Day 21:**

**Review day**  
Check fluency improvement  
Note 3 habits to remove  
Note 3 strong improvements

---

## 🗓️ **WEEK 4 — Break the Freeze and Speak Confidently**

_Goal: turn speaking into a natural, daily habit._

### **Day 22:**

Shadowing 10 minutes  
Practice “speaking fast” for 30 seconds

### **Day 23:**

Conversation simulation with me  
Real-life situations (restaurant, work, meeting)

### **Day 24:**

Build your personal speech:  
Introduce yourself  
Describe your job  
Describe your strengths in English

### **Day 25:**

Learn 10 filler words:  
“Let me think…”  
“You know…”  
“Basically…”  
These help you speak smoothly.

### **Day 26:**

Talk 3 minutes nonstop  
Even if you pause, don’t switch to Vietnamese.

### **Day 27:**

Storytelling day  
Tell a story from your childhood or travel

### **Day 28:**

Shadowing + reading aloud  
Focus on pronunciation

### **Day 29:**

Record a 3–4 minute audio  
Topic: “My English journey and my goals”

### **Day 30:**

🎉 **Final check**  
Speak for 5 minutes without planning  
You will hear a huge difference.
