## **✨ 1. Phrases to Start Speaking (when your mind is blank)**

Use these to buy time and think.
1. **“Well…”**
2. **“Let’s see…”**
3. **“Hmm… good question.”**
4. **“Honestly…”**
5. **“Let me try to explain.”**
---
## **✨ 2. Phrases to Continue Speaking (keep the sentence flowing)**
These help your speaking sound natural.
6. **“So basically…”**
7. **“What I mean is…”**
8. **“You know…”**
9. **“Actually…”**
10. **“I guess…”**

---
## **✨ 3. Phrases to Share Opinions**
Perfect for work and conversation.
11. **“In my opinion…”**
12. **“From my point of view…”**
13. **“I feel like…”**
14. **“As far as I know…”**
15. **“I’d say that…”**

---
## **✨ 4. Phrases to Clarify or Correct Yourself**
Useful when you make a mistake or want to fix a sentence.
16. **“Sorry, I mean…”**
17. **“Let me rephrase that.”**
18. **“What I wanted to say is…”**

---
## **✨ 5. Phrases to End or Conclude Thoughts**
These help you finish your idea confidently.
19. **“So yeah, that’s it.”**
20. **“That’s what I think.”**
