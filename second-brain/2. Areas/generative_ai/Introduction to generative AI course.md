# genrative AI vs discriminate AI
When generative AI has provided data about cat and can generate cat photo, the discriminate AI will be provide both cat and dogs photo (input) and their task is distinguish which one is cat photo and which one is dog photos.

There was a contest that using those AI. First generative AI was given task to generate image and dicriminate AI has to point out which one is generated, and from those both AIs get better.
