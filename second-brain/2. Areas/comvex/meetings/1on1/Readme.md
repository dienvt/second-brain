## Agenda
1. 🧊 Icebreaker & Personal Check-in (5 mins)  
_Start on a human level to build rapport and gauge their current energy levels._  

- How are things going outside of work?
- How are you feeling about your workload and work-life balance this week?
- What was the highlight of your weekend/week?

2. 🗣️ Team Member's Floor  
_Give them ownership of the meeting first. This shows you value their priorities._  

- **Top of mind:** What are the most important things you’d like to discuss today?
- **Wins & Achievements:** What are you most proud of since we last spoke?
- **Roadblocks:** Is anything slowing you down or causing frustration? How can I help remove these blockers?
- **Support:** Do you have the resources, tools, and clarity you need right now?

3. ⛳️ Manager's Updates & Feedback  
_Share your observations, align on goals, and cascade important information._  

- **Feedback:** Share one piece of positive feedback and one area for constructive improvement (if applicable).
- **Alignment:** Review progress on current sprint/quarterly goals.
- **Company/Team Updates:** Share any relevant news, priority shifts, or upcoming projects they should be aware of.

4. 🚀Career Growth & Development  
_Keep long-term goals in sight, even during routine catch-ups._  

- Are you finding your current tasks engaging and challenging?
- Is there a new skill you'd like to learn or a project you'd like to take on soon?
- Are we still on track with your long-term career goals?

5. ✅ Action Items & Wrap-up  
_Ensure both of you leave with clear expectations._  

- Summarize key takeaways from the meeting.
- **Manager Action Items:** What do I owe you before next time?
- **Team Member Action Items:** What will you be focusing on?A
