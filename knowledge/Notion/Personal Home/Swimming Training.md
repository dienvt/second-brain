**1. Perceived Exertion:**

- On a scale of 1 to 10 (with 1 being the easiest and 10 the hardest), a moderate pace usually feels like a 5 or 6. It's a pace at which you're working harder than your easy, relaxed pace but not pushing so hard that you can't sustain the effort for a while.

**2. Talk Test:**

- At a moderate pace, you should be able to speak a few words or a short sentence but not be able to comfortably hold a conversation.

**3. Use a Baseline Set:**

- Swim a known distance (e.g., 500 meters or yards) at what you feel is your maximum sustainable pace. Time yourself.
- Your moderate pace would typically be around 10-20% slower than this time.
    
    For example: If you swam 500 meters in 10 minutes at your max sustainable pace, your moderate 500m pace might be around 11 to 12 minutes.
