# Header

As a manager, it is important to have a clear roadmap to guide your team towards success. Here are some key steps to building an effective roadmap:

## 1. Define Your Goals

The first step in building a roadmap is to clearly define your goals. This means identifying what you want to achieve as a team, and breaking those goals down into manageable steps.

## 2. Prioritize Your Tasks

Once you have your goals defined, it's important to prioritize your tasks. This means determining which tasks are most important and urgent, and which can be tackled later.

## 3. Assign Tasks to Team Members

With your tasks prioritized, you can assign them to team members. It's important to consider each team member's strengths and weaknesses when assigning tasks, to ensure that everyone is working on tasks they are suited for.

## 4. Set Deadlines

Deadlines are critical to ensuring that tasks are completed on time. Set realistic deadlines for each task, and make sure that team members are aware of these deadlines.

## 5. Monitor Progress

As tasks are being completed, it's important to monitor progress. This means checking in with team members regularly to see how they are progressing, and making adjustments as needed.

## 6. Celebrate Successes

Finally, it's important to celebrate successes along the way. This means recognizing when team members have achieved their goals, and taking time to acknowledge and celebrate these achievements.

By following these steps, you can build an effective roadmap that will guide your team towards success. Good luck!

**The process:**

1. Write out all possible options.
2. Cross out what won’t work.
3. Write pros and cons, come up with a preference, and discuss with your team.

**If you’re still unsure, do any of the following:**

- Act as your end-user and critique each solution from that perspective.
- Have 1:1 discussions with someone more junior and someone more senior.
- Consider how reversible the decision is, what the cost of each one being wrong is, and what room each one has for being extended in the future.

  

# Framework

## GOAL setting

### SMART principle

- **S**pecific (simple, sensible, significant): **Tính cụ thể, dễ hiểu**
- **M**easurable (meaningful, motivating): **Có thể đo lường được**
- **A**ttainable (agreed, achievable): **Tính khả thi**
- **R**ealistic (reasonable, realistic and resourced, results-based): **Tính khả thi**
- **T**ime-base (timely, time-sensitive): **thiết lập thời gian**
